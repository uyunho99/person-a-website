How we work

We uncover unspoken needs, understand hidden pain points, and design experiences that truly matter.

01

Read the context

Behavior only makes sense in context — when, where, under what pressure, and instead of what. We read the situation, not just the click.

02

Find the unmet needs

The most valuable needs are the ones customers never say. We find them in the gap between what people do and what they are offered.

03

Design what matters

Every insight ends in a direction for the experience — what to build, change or stop — not a profile to file away.

What you get

Each profile is emitted as artifacts a team can act on

The persona card reports measured stability per dimension. Its reverse face carries handling rules derived from those measurements. The store card passes the open question to the floor. The timing panel is calculated from the observed return curve. All regenerate when the behaviour moves.

The Decision Surfer
Do not add candidates. Resolve the ones already open.
Do not read a brand switch as churn. Taste held at 0.974 while brands changed 2.71 times.

In store, build conviction. Load her own shortlist, never new stock.
